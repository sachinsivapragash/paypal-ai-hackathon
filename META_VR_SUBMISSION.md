### Inspiration
Freelancers lose hours every week chasing payments. The work is small but constant: noticing who is late, nudging them, checking what came in. Spreadsheets hide the urgency, because a $2,400 overdue invoice looks the same as a $40 one. We wanted a short daily ritual you could do from a sofa, a train seat or a desk: put on the headset, *see* what you are owed, clear the urgent items with your hands, and take the headset off in under five minutes.

### What it does
- **Your receivables as a physical shelf.** Each unpaid invoice is a block. Height shows the amount; colour shows the status (amber waiting, red overdue, green paid). Blocks sit on a timeline arc, with the most overdue on the left, and overdue blocks pulse with red alarm rings.
- **Hands-first, seated.** Pinch an invoice to pick it up, point at a bin (**Remind**, **Cancel** or **Sync status**) and let go. There is no throwing, standing or walking: everything works within arm's reach of a chair. Throwing also works, for fun.
- **Nothing happens without your approval.** A bin only *proposes* an action. A small tablet appears on your desk; drop it on **APPROVE** to run it, or on **Reject** to discard it.
- **In-reach console.** Physical buttons for **Chase overdue** (proposes a reminder for every late invoice), a weekly **Briefing**, **Match payments**, and one-pinch quick questions to the assistant: *Who should I chase first?*, *Cash summary*, *Unpaid list*. Answers are spoken aloud.
- **Glanceable panels above the shelf:** totals, a 4-week cash forecast, client risk orbs, an audit log and a combo meter that rewards clearing work quickly.
- **Mixed reality that uses your room.** In passthrough, a reticle tracks your real floor; pinch to place the ledger in front of your actual desk, and use **📍 Move ledger** to put it somewhere else.
- **Fits your posture.** On entering VR it measures your eye height and moves the room down for seated use, keeping the shelf just below your hands.
- **Opening in under a minute:** a five-step "Get started" panel ticks off as you pinch, drop, approve, ask and chase. If your account is empty it opens with clearly labelled sample data, so there is always something to do.
- **Clean pause and resume:** syncing stops while the headset or page is hidden and resumes when you return.

### How we built it
- A WebXR app written with three.js (`immersive-vr` and `immersive-ar`), using hand tracking, haptics and hit-testing. Interaction is ray-based pinch: grab on pinch, drop by pointing at a target on release, so every action works from a fixed seat.
- Layout is designed around a narrow field of view: the shelf, bins and console sit in front of you, and secondary panels sit slightly above.
- A Node.js backend holds invoices, enforces the approve-before-run rule on every write, keeps an audit log, and runs the assistant. All money-related logic, limits and approvals live in our own code.
- Tested in Meta's Immersive Web Emulator (IWER) as a Quest 3 using **hand tracking only**. The demo video is recorded there, seated, with no controllers.

### Challenges
- Making every action work from a chair. Throwing to a floor-level bin is fun standing up and impossible seated, so release-while-pointing became the main gesture.
- Buttons sitting in front of grabbable objects: rays initially grabbed the invoice *behind* a button. The nearest hit now wins.
- Seated users see the room from below; automatic height fitting fixed that.

### Accomplishments
A complete daily loop with hands only: see what's late, propose, approve, ask, and finish, in about two minutes, with every action logged and nothing executed without the user's say-so.

### What's next
- Scene understanding: snap the ledger to your real desk surface rather than the floor, and remember its position between sessions with spatial anchors.
- Gaze-plus-pinch selection, and a high-contrast mode.
- A morning streak, so the five-minute ritual becomes a habit.
- Multi-user: an accountant joins your room to review the same ledger.

**Target launch date:** Q1 2027 (public beta in the Meta Quest Browser), then a store build.

## Testing instructions for judges
1. Open https://tallyspace-vr.onrender.com in the Meta Quest Browser. If it is slow the first time, wait up to about 50 seconds for the server to wake.
2. Press **Enter VR** (or **Start AR** for passthrough), put the controllers down and use your hands.
3. Follow the **Get started** panel: pinch a red invoice, point at **Remind**, release; pinch the tablet that appears and release it over **APPROVE**.
4. Press **Chase overdue** and a quick-question button on the right.
5. In AR: look at the floor in front of your desk and pinch to place the ledger.
6. The room shows sample data, and nothing is sent anywhere.
